Yamaha C5 grand piano samples recorded by Alexander Holm.
These mp3 files are the per-note set distributed with Tone.js (https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/),
sampled every minor third. Only A1 ... Ds6 are included (the merge game's piano pieces use C#2 ... B5).
Used by static/js/merge/music.js (머지관 배경음악 피아노).
